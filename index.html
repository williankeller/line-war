      Rules

      1. Fighters bounce around the arena at a constant speed.
      2. Touching the wall makes a fighter grip it and slide along the ring. Every degree it slides adds one line anchored to the wall. Grazing hits slide longer and earn more lines.
      3. A rival whose body crosses a line cuts it.
      4. A fighter that loses its last line explodes.
      5. In sudden death, grips get short and everyone speeds up, so the match always ends.

      Press Space to start, pause or resume.
